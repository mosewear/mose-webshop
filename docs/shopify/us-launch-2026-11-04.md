This branch is not approval to activate US sales or Meta spend. Keep existing Netherlands and Belgium EUR checkouts intact.

## Verified blockers
- The custom MOSE website uses Next.js 16 and Supabase with a separate Shopify checkout integration.
- Shopify has a DRAFT United States market with USD market currency. The store-wide list of enabled payment/presentment currencies was still only EUR. Shopify Payments account status was unavailable with current API permissions.
- The Next.js cart snapshot hardcodes country NL; the server creates Shopify carts with buyer country NL and validates merchandise prices, discounts and totals in EUR. Changing a single field to US would cause mismatches and could create incorrect payable totals.
- A real checkout test on checkout.mosewear.com offered only Netherlands and Belgium; USD, a US shipping quote, and duties were not available.
- In MOSE Admin the Shopify cutover had 30 of 32 checks passing; coupon parity and verified stock by location remain open. Do not manually set verification flags without evidence.
- The shipping profile MOSE — Tapstitch Nederland covers the new products, but had no US zone. Tapstitch Special Line had a US zone but no confirmed assigned products. General shipping profile had a US shipping zone for different products.
- Every checked physical Shopify product variant had missing or unconfirmed declared origin and HS code. These must be verified by actual manufacturer and composition.
- RW0016-C001-V3 is the selected 430 GSM oversized heavyweight replacement for RW0041 cropped fleece. Exact back-print safe dimensions, alignment and available color names were not confirmed in Tapstitch's public details.
- The Midnight Bloom Shopify retail price was set to EUR 89 on all variants. Old Boxy Fleece and Midnight Bloom Shopify entries were set to DRAFT to block incorrect old-garment orders; the custom MOSE catalog requires independent review.
- The Meta campaign remains paused, with four tracked purchase-optimized ads.

## Written Tapstitch response received 2026-11-04, replaces assumptions
Tapstitch support responded to the RW0016-specific questions. They confirmed RW0016 starts at **USD 22.99** per unit, with **USD 2.99 per printed area** and **1–3 business days production**. Available colors (13 advertised), exact print sizes/margins, shipping/handling costs, duties, and final delivery charges vary by configuration and location. Support expressly **cannot provide a universal confirmation that duties, taxes and all landed charges are included**. The design tool supplies the SKU-specific printable surface; they provide no additional downloadable template or neckline margins. Existing Shopify supplier variant mappings cannot be guaranteed safe when replacing garments. Mixed-order eligibility depends on specific shipping and carrier choices. Consequently **do not activate MOSE_US_DDP_VERIFIED or MOSE_US_SHIPPING_RATES_VERIFIED**, even though a generic Tapstitch help page describes a DDP model. The fully specified Tapstitch test checkout is required and recipient-surcharges must be contractually covered by MOSE before any 'no surprise fees' promise. The ODMPOD name in Shopify refers to the Tapstitch supplier platform, but separately owned Groningen stock uses its own carrier.
